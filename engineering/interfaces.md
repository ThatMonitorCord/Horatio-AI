# Conversation, media & Minecraft

[Hub](../README.md) · My engineering notebook · 21 October 2026

## Discord text

I establish message eligibility and conversation scope, ingest eligible history, retrieve context and produce a response through the public output path. I preserve personality alongside evidence requirements. Horatio can be opinionated about coffee; a factual report still needs support.

## Voice

I connect received audio, speech recognition, scoped reasoning, synthesis and queued playback. I support wake-word handling and voice-specific response limits. I serialize speech chunks to preserve their order. I measure transcription and audible delivery separately; a successful transcript is only one stage.

## Images and video

I bound attachment size and decoded dimensions before analysis. For video I sample frames; I do not claim continuous perception of every moment or understanding of the full soundtrack. I distinguish content analysis from each occurrence of an attachment, so a cached analysis does not erase where the media appeared.

## Context accounting

I exclude encoded image transport from text accounting and estimate visual cost separately. I reserve output space and safety headroom before admitting input. This prevents a large encoded image string from consuming the conversation allowance. I have local image and frame checks; real Discord attachment receipt-to-reply validation remains outstanding.

## Server evidence and gameplay

I connect timestamped telemetry, player history, server-health observations and bounded historical queries. I preserve freshness and coverage information: an empty query during incomplete ingestion proves little. I keep private evidence behind scoped access and public summaries. I treat operator-started gameplay as a separate experimental path, checking observed state after navigation, crafting or other actions.
